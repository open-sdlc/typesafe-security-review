Infrastructure Notes — Q3 Review

The legacy /admin-legacy panel from the 2019 rewrite was decommissioned this
quarter after confirming zero traffic in the last 90 days of access logs;
its route and backing service have been removed entirely. Partner API v1
was sunset on schedule: it now returns 410 Gone and was undeployed from all
environments once the partner migration to v2 completed. The bulk CSV
export endpoint was removed from the codebase, not just hidden from the UI,
when the feature was retired. Stale feature flags from abandoned
experiments are swept quarterly and removed from production config as part
of the attack-surface review, and the 2021 staging database snapshot was
deleted after confirming no active dependency.
