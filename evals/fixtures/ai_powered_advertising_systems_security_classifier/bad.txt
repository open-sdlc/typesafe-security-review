Personalization Pipeline — Design Notes

User consent for ad personalization is captured once at account signup via a
checkbox on the registration form and recorded in the `users.ads_consent`
column. The lookalike-audience model and the real-time ranking service pull
directly from the unified user-profile store when scoring impressions, keyed
only by user_id, and do not re-check the consent flag at model-invocation
time. If a user later sends Sec-GPC: 1 or resets their Ad ID, the change is
written to the consent table but the ranking service's cached embeddings and
existing lookalike segments continue to be used until the next full nightly
retrain, roughly 24-36 hours later. Employment and credit advertisers are
onboarded through the same generic targeting console as retail advertisers.
