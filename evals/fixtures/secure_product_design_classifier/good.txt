Design Doc: Vendor Invoice Automation Service v1

Summary
The new invoicing service splits invoice creation from invoice approval and
payment issuance across two distinct service accounts, enforcing separation
of duties end to end.

Proposed roles
- InvoiceDrafterBot: can create draft invoices and set line-item amounts.
  Cannot approve invoices or initiate payments.
- PaymentApproverBot: can approve invoices above a threshold only after a
  human finance reviewer signs off in the UI, and is the only account
  permitted to call the payment API.

Data access
InvoiceDrafterBot's database credential has write access only to the
invoices table. PaymentApproverBot has read access to invoices and write
access only to the payments table, scoped via least-privilege IAM policies.

Rollout plan
Ship v1 with both roles and the human approval step in place from day one;
this is a hard requirement for launch, not a v2 follow-up.
