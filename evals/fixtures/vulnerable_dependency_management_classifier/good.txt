Dependency Alert Triage Notes - lodash 4.17.15 (CVE-2020-8203, prototype pollution)

`npm audit` flagged lodash 4.17.15 as vulnerable to prototype pollution. We
upgraded to lodash 4.17.21, the patched release, and updated the two
internal build scripts that depended on the old behavior.

Before merging, we added a regression test that reproduces the
CVE-2020-8203 payload against `_.zipObjectDeep`: it fails against the
pinned 4.17.15 install and passes once the upgrade lands, confirming this
is a real fix rather than a suppressed scanner alert. `npm audit` now
reports zero known vulnerabilities for the dependency tree.
