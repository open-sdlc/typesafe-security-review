The API gateway validates the caller's JWT and forwards the request,
but every internal service also independently verifies the JWT
signature and claims before acting -- authorization is never granted
on gateway trust alone. All service-to-service traffic inside the
cluster runs over mTLS issued by the internal service mesh's
certificate authority, so order-service, payment-service, and
ledger-service each authenticate the calling service's certificate on
every request in addition to re-checking the caller's identity.
