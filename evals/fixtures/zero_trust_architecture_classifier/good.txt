Corporate Network Access Policy

- Every request, whether from inside or outside the office network, must
  present a valid short-lived device certificate and user token; network
  location alone grants no trust, and internal traffic is authenticated
  the same as traffic arriving from the public internet.
- Production database access is granted just-in-time: an engineer
  requests elevated access for a specific task, it is approved and
  auto-expires after 4 hours, and standing admin credentials are not
  issued to anyone.
- Internal services authenticate each other via short-lived mTLS
  certificates tied to a service identity, re-verified on every request,
  rather than relying on which subnet a connection originated from.
