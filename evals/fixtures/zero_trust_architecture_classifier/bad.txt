Corporate Network Access Policy

- Employees connect to the corporate VPN once per week; once connected,
  the VPN grants access to the entire internal 10.0.0.0/8 network, since
  anything "inside" the VPN is considered trusted.
- Engineers are issued standing admin credentials to the production
  database on their first day and keep them for as long as they're
  employed, so they don't need to request access every time.
- Internal services authenticate each other using IP allowlists scoped to
  the internal subnet; no additional per-request identity check is
  performed because the network itself is treated as the trust boundary.
