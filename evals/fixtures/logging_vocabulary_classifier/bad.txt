Logging design notes -- Identity Service

Every login attempt emits an AUTHN_login_success or AUTHN_login_failure
event with the username, source IP, and timestamp. After five
consecutive AUTHN_login_failure events for the same account within ten
minutes, an AUTHN_login_lock event is emitted and the account is
locked. Password changes emit AUTHN_password_change, and a login from
a country more than 1,000 km from the account's previous login within
an implausible time window emits AUTHN_impossible_travel so the fraud
team can review it.
