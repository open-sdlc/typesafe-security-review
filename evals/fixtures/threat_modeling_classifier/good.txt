Threat Model: Customer Portal Login Service

Identified threats and response decisions:

1. Attacker steals a valid session cookie via XSS and reuses it to
   impersonate the victim.
   Response: Mitigate. Requirement: set HttpOnly/Secure/SameSite on session
   cookies and add CSP headers; ticket SEC-101, owner: platform team.

2. Attacker submits crafted requests that update another user's shipping
   address without authorization.
   Response: Mitigate. Requirement: enforce per-object authorization checks
   server-side on every mutation endpoint; ticket SEC-102, owner: API team.

3. Attacker floods the /login endpoint with failed attempts, locking out
   legitimate users' accounts.
   Response: Mitigate. Requirement: rate-limit by IP and account, add
   CAPTCHA after 5 failures; ticket SEC-103, owner: auth team.

4. Attacker modifies the "role" claim in a JWT to gain admin access.
   Response: Mitigate. Requirement: sign JWTs with RS256 and verify the
   signature on every request; ticket SEC-104, owner: auth team.

5. Attacker deletes audit log entries after tampering with an order.
   Response: Accept for v1, revisit in Q3. Rationale: audit logs are
   shipped to a write-only external log sink within 5 seconds, limiting
   the blast radius; documented and signed off by security lead.

All five items above have been reviewed by the security lead and are
tracked to closure, with SEC-101 through SEC-104 already in this sprint.
