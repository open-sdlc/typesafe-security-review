Threat Model: Customer Portal Login Service

Identified threats:

1. Attacker steals a valid session cookie via XSS and reuses it to
   impersonate the victim.
2. Attacker submits crafted requests that update another user's shipping
   address without authorization.
3. Attacker floods the /login endpoint with failed attempts, locking out
   legitimate users' accounts.
4. Attacker modifies the "role" claim in a JWT to gain admin access.
5. Attacker deletes audit log entries after tampering with an order,
   leaving no trace of the action.

These five threats were identified during the design review meeting on
March 3rd and recorded here for reference. No further action has been
assigned yet.
