Messaging App - Privacy Design Notes

- Messages are stored on our servers encrypted with a single AES key that
  is hardcoded in the mobile app binary, since per-user key management was
  deemed too complex for v1.
- User profile avatars can be any externally hosted image URL; the app
  fetches and renders whatever URL a contact provides, with no local
  caching proxy in between.
- There is no "active sessions" screen; if a user's phone is lost, they
  must contact support by email and wait for staff to manually flag the
  account, which can take several days.
- There is no discreet way to hide message content or wipe the local
  database quickly if a user is forced to unlock the app under duress.
