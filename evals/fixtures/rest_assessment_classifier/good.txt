The orders service publishes a complete OpenAPI 3.0 specification at
/api/v1/openapi.json, listing every supported route, method, and parameter,
including GET /api/v1/orders, GET /api/v1/orders/{id}, and
POST /api/v1/orders/{id}/refund (the latter requires an `admin` scope and
is documented as internal-only). There are no undocumented parameters or
hidden debug flags; the client SPA's calls match the spec one-for-one, and
a nightly job diffs the live service's routes against the spec to catch any
drift before it ships.
