Account Recovery Setup

For account recovery, we no longer rely on security questions alone. Choose
at least one of the following, and we will also require a one-time code
sent to your verified email or phone before granting access:

- Enter the last four digits of a payment method on file (verified against
  our records, not typed freely).
- Confirm a randomly selected transaction amount from your last 90 days of
  account activity.
- Provide the answer to a question you pre-registered from a vetted,
  high-entropy list; the answer is stored using the same salted hashing
  algorithm as your password, never in plaintext.

Recovery cannot be completed using a security-question answer alone; a
second verified factor is always required, and answers are never reused as
the sole gate to reset your password.
