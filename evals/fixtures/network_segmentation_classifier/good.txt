Network Policy -- Customer Portal

The environment is split into three firewalled zones: FRONTEND (load
balancer, 10.0.1.0/26), MIDDLEWARE (application servers, 10.0.2.0/26),
and BACKEND (database, 10.0.3.0/26). The load balancer may only reach
the application servers on port 443; only the application servers may
reach the database, on port 5432. Outbound Internet access from
MIDDLEWARE and BACKEND is denied by default except for an explicit
allow-list of patch-update endpoints. Logs are forwarded in real time
to a separate append-only log segment that none of the three zones can
write to directly.
